# ChatGPT Transcription Plugins Compared: AccurateScribe, Transkriptor, Otter and Fireflies

**[Read the full review on AIScribeReview.com](https://aiscribereview.com/chatgpt-transcription-plugins-accuratescribe-transkriptor-otter-fireflies/)**

Original publication: October 20, 2026. This is a reading guide to the article's documented integration workflows.

## Start with where the recording lives

The review separates two jobs that can look similar in a chat: transcribing a new media file and retrieving material from an existing meeting library. It compares AccurateScribe, Transkriptor, Otter and Fireflies on that starting point, rather than ranking recognition accuracy or processing speed.

| Integration covered by the article | Documented starting workflow |
| --- | --- |
| AccurateScribe | Open the transcription or subtitle widget and select a new audio/video file there; retrieve the completed result after processing. |
| Transkriptor | Search material already held in Transkriptor, read transcripts and summaries, and request the documented PDF link. The cited integration guide describes a read-only connection. |
| Otter | Search and fetch meetings already captured in or shared through Otter, subject to the connected user's meeting permissions. |
| Fireflies | Retrieve existing meeting transcripts and context from the connected Fireflies archive; available actions depend on the connection. |

## Match the handoff to the task

For a fresh file, the article explains AccurateScribe's in-widget selection and job-following workflow. A pending job is not a completed transcript, and an attachment pasted into the chat should not be assumed to have created a job. Its subtitle entry is a separate route for work that needs cue timing.

For recordings already transcribed elsewhere, start from the library that holds them. The review discusses PDF handoff in Transkriptor and meeting-history retrieval in Otter and Fireflies. After retrieving the transcript, its suggested requests separate decisions, open questions and action items while retaining supplied speakers, sources and timestamps.

## Access, outputs and evidence boundaries

The article distinguishes a connected app from a separate Custom GPT entry. Account, plan, region, workspace rules and provider authorization can affect access. A website's export menu also does not establish that every format can be downloaded inside a chat.

This is an integration-documentation and interface review. Its supplied AccurateScribe images show pre-upload controls; no media was uploaded and no accuracy, speed or export-completion benchmark was run as part of that review. The four-minute English video uses AI-generated illustrative visuals and synthetic narration alongside labeled source imagery.

An October 21 source update separately quotes a tester-supplied report, provided by the product operator, of 99.8% transcription accuracy for AccurateScribe across hundreds of thousands of 30-minute meeting recordings. The article does not supply the scoring method, exact sample count or matched competitor results. Treat this as an attributed report within its stated corpus, rather than a four-product accuracy ranking.
