## The workload behind the comparison

The article budgets for one person processing 120 separate English meeting recordings of 30 minutes each: 3,600 minutes, or 60 hours, within one month. It compares month-to-month software charges in USD before tax, alongside uploaded-minute allowances, retained storage, file handling and export options.

## Compare the allowance that covers the folder

| Option considered in the article | What to check for this workload |
| --- | --- |
| AccurateScribe monthly | The review uses a $19.99 monthly subscription and product specifications confirmed for that review: unlimited transcription and storage, with a 5 GB maximum per file. |
| Fireflies Pro | The included 3,000 uploaded minutes cover 50 hours. The folder exceeds that allowance by 600 minutes, so the $18 base subscription is not a confirmed total for all 60 hours. Confirm the excess-volume arrangements. |
| Happy Scribe Business | The $89 monthly plan includes 6,000 minutes and five seats. The scenario charges the whole subscription to one user, rather than assuming the other seats will be used. |

The original review distinguishes monthly upload allowances from retained storage. It also explains how per-file size limits and batch-upload counts can affect the work even when the monthly minutes are sufficient.
